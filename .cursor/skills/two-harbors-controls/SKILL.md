# Controls (one scheme)

PLAN §3.10. Mouse and finger map to the same two actions.

| Action | Meaning | Mouse | Finger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Ground: walk. Thing: use (walk into range). | Left click | Tap |
| Secondary | Extra menu. Never required to shop. | Right click (suppress browser menu) | Long-press ~400ms |

HUD buttons are the same widgets on a 27-inch monitor and a phone.

**Exceptions already shipped:** taxi exit is a HUD/map control, not left-click,
because left-click must keep meaning walk/use.

`input-systems` rebinding/gamepad: later. Do not add WASD "for desktop".
