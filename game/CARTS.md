# Street carts — grind, warehouse, look

Player-facing loop for 2Isles street carts. Sim owns cash, stock, scores. Browser is a camera.

## Ladder (tied to land)

Starter cash **$1,000**. Highway cart pad **$750**. South street floor **$2,400**. North street **$6,800**.

| Cart | Kit | After a pad? | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fruit | $90 + pack $14 | yes (~$854 spent) | The start. 1/10 empty. |
| Watermelon | $1,150 | no | First grind cart. 1/10 empty. |
| Fish and chips | $1,850 | no | Premium cart. 2/10 empty. Games stay as they are. |

Hire $300 is already after the first fruit sales. Upgrades that push a cart from 1–2/10 toward 8–10/10 cost on the order of a South street lot, not a fridge snack.

## Buy path

Marketplace **Add Cart** → pay → **South warehouse**. Kerb van stays an option on the pay sheet. Warehouse lists kits with Place, stock with Bring to me.

## Mini-game sales

A finished Play shift sells a burst from the cart. If the cart is empty, pull stock that is already in the warehouse. If there is still nothing to sell, the status says why (empty, no propane, cooldown) — you do not play 24s for silence. Fish and chips games are left alone. Fruit and watermelon each get a second tap game.
